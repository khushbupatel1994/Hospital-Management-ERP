# High-Profile Hospital Demo Checklist

## Demo flow

- Login with role-based access
- Dashboard KPI overview
- Patient registration and search
- Doctor and appointment workflow
- OPD/IPD workflow
- Laboratory workflow
- Pharmacy and inventory workflow
- Billing and reports
- Analytics
- AI Intelligence Center
- AI operational question demo
- Backup/security demonstration

## Recommended demo questions

- How many patients are registered?
- How many appointments are pending?
- How many appointments are scheduled today?
- How many laboratory tests are pending?
- Which inventory areas need attention?
- How many active IPD admissions are recorded?
- What is the recorded billing revenue?

## Positioning

Present the system as an **AI-Powered Hospital Management & Intelligence Platform**. For a first sale, describe the AI features as administrative and operational decision-support, not autonomous clinical care.
