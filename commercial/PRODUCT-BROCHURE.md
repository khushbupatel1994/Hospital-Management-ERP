# Cloud Secure Hospital ERP — Product Overview

## Positioning
An enterprise-oriented Hospital Management and Intelligence Platform combining hospital workflows, centralized APIs, role-based security, auditability, analytics, and controlled AI assistance.

## Core Modules
- Patient Management
- Doctor Management
- Appointments
- OPD/IPD
- Laboratory
- Pharmacy
- Inventory
- Billing & Accounts
- HR/User Management
- Reports & Analytics
- Security & Audit
- AI Hospital Assistant

## Enterprise Capabilities
- Central API architecture
- RBAC and session security
- MFA foundation
- Audit trail
- Notifications
- PostgreSQL-ready production database
- Redis-ready session/revocation architecture
- Backup and recovery tooling
- Health monitoring
- HTTPS/TLS deployment architecture

## AI Capabilities
AI is designed for operational assistance, summaries, search, insights, and controlled data workflows. It is not positioned as an autonomous diagnosis or prescribing system.

## Deployment Model
Hospital desktops run the client application while protected services and data are hosted on controlled infrastructure.
