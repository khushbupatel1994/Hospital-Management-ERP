# Incident Response Runbook

1. Detect and classify the event.
2. Contain affected account, endpoint or service without destroying evidence.
3. Preserve relevant logs and timestamps.
4. Escalate to the hospital security/IT owner.
5. Investigate scope and affected data.
6. Apply remediation and rotate exposed credentials.
7. Recover from a verified clean state.
8. Complete required notifications/reporting based on applicable law and hospital policy.
9. Perform a post-incident review and update controls.

Keep an incident record with: incident ID, timestamps, systems affected, actions taken, evidence locations, decisions, approvals and closure date.
